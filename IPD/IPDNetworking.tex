\section{Consider the strength of your relationship}

Strong ties are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item People who have a long history of reciprocation;
    \item People you trust;
    \item People you interact with frequently;
    \item People you see in many different domains of your life
\end{itemize}

Weak ties are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Acquaintances you don't know all that well
    \item People you've only known for a short white or in limited contexts
    \item People with whom you interact infrequently
\end{itemize}

Categorize each person in your list as a strong or weak tie.

\section{Analyze your network map}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Looking at your network map, what patterns do you see?
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Look for structural holes, or boxes in the map that have few or no names
        \item Notice if one person shows up in several categories to the exclusion of other contacts.
        In this case, you may be too reliant on one member of your network for multiple purposes. 
        Ideally, you will have a combination of strong and weak ties in each of the boxes.
    \end{itemize}
